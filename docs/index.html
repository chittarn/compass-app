LEGAL COMPLIANCE & DATA SAFETY

Privacy Policy

Application: Compass (Tactical Navigation & Inclinometer) • Effective Date: November 15, 2026

1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Your privacy is paramount. Compass is designed with an on-device architecture. The app functions as an offline instrument tool: your sensors, heading calculations, attitude angles, and navigation data are computed purely in local memory on your mobile device.

We do not harvest, track, record, or transmit your GPS coordinates, sensor readings, or personal information.

2. Permissions Requested & Purpose

  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION):
    Used strictly in real-time to compute the geographic offset between Magnetic North and True North (magnetic declination using the World Magnetic Model) and to display current altitude (elevation MSL). Your coordinates are processed in volatile RAM and are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • Hardware Sensors (MAGNETOMETER & ACCELEROMETER):
    Used to calculate device heading, orientation, pitch, and roll for the 360° compass rose and 2D artificial horizon bubble level.
  • Haptics & Vibration (VIBRATE):
    Used to provide physical tactile feedback when crossing cardinal directions and the North azimuth.

3. Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

The free version of Compass displays non-intrusive banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect and process standard anonymous device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy to serve relevant ads.

Users who purchase the Pro Tier receive a 100% ad-free experience with all advertising SDK activity completely disabled.

4. Data Retention & Security

Because we do not operate telemetry servers or store user databases, no user data is retained by us. All user preferences (such as True North mode or Pro upgrade status) are stored locally on your device via Android SharedPreferences.
